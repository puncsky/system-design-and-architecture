# `.pm` anti-goals — do not do

Hard constraints. `/pm-brainstorm`, `/pm` and `/loopx` read this and must reject conflicting items explicitly.

- **No secrets or private material.** Nothing credential-like, no private repo contents, no employer-confidential system details in chapters or on the board.
- **No fabricated facts.** No invented benchmarks, scale numbers, quotes, or references. Cite a source or label a figure as an estimate.
- **No one-language changes.** Don't add, rename, or restructure a chapter in only `en/` or only `zh-CN/`; mirror filenames and front matter.
- **No renumbering.** Never rename existing `<number>-<slug>.md` files or change `slug`/`id`; they are stable IDs shared with tianpan.co.
- **No plagiarism.** Don't copy third-party text or diagrams; summarize, attribute in `references`, and draw originals.
- **No spammy growth tactics.** No star-begging, bulk unsolicited issues/PRs elsewhere, SEO filler, or link-farming in `awesome-system-design.md`.
- **No generated filler.** Don't pad chapters with generic AI prose; every section needs concrete tradeoffs, numbers or a diagram.
- **No milestone theater.** If it isn't >1h of real work with an observable reader outcome, it's an inbox note or nothing.
